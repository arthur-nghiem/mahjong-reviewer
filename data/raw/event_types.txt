event-types.txt: documents all event types and associated variables in the mjai protocol

"ankan": a player calls a concealed quad
    actor (int): the player making the call
    pai (str): which tile the player has four of
    consumed (list[str]): the four tiles being revealed

"chi": a player makes a call to complete a sequence from an opponent's discard
    actor (int): the player who takes the tile
    target (int): the player who discarded the tile
    pai (str): the discarded tile
    consumed (list[str]): the two other tiles needed to complete the sequence

"dahai": a player discards one of their tiles
    actor (int): the player who discards the tile
    pai (str): the tile the player discards
    tsumogiri (bool): true if and only if the player discards the tile they just drew

"daiminkan": a player calls an open quad
    actor (int): the player making the call
    pai (str): which tile the player is taking
    consumed (list[str]): the existing three tiles being revealed

"dora": a new dora tile is revealed
    dora_marker (str): the tile revealed in the dead wall

"end_game": all rounds have been completed

"end_kyoku": an individual round has been completed

"hora": a player declares a win in the current round
    actor (int): the player who achieves the winning hand
    target (int): the player who drew the winning tile
    deltas (list[int]): score changes for each of the four players
    ura_markers (list[str]): tiles that appear as ura markers

"kakan": a player calls an added quad
    actor (int): the player making the call
    pai (str): the added tile
    consumed (list[str]): the existing open triplet

"pon": a player makes a call to complete a triplet from an opponent's discard
    actor (int): the player who takes the tile
    target (int): the player who discarded the tile
    pai (str): the discarded tile
    consumed (list[str]): the two other tiles needed to complete the triplet

"reach": a player declares riichi
    actor (int): the player who declares riichi

"reach_accepted": a player who has declared riichi does not lose off of their discard, and places riichi stick
    actor (int): the player who places the 1,000 point riichi bet

"ryukyoku": an exhaustive draw occurs
    deltas (list[int]): score changes for each of the four players

"start_game": the beginning of the game
    names (list[str]): the players in the game
    kyoku_first (int): always 0
    aka_flag (bool): always true

"start_kyoku": the beginning of a round
    bakaze (str): the round wind
    dora_marker (str): the tile initially revealed in the dead wall
    kyoku (int): the round number, ranging from 1 to 4
    honba (int): the repeat counter
    kyotaku (int): riichi sticks left over from the previous round
    oya (int): the dealer position, always one less than kyoku
    scores (list[int]): the current scores of each player
    tehais (list[list[str]]): starting hand of 13 tiles for each of the four players

"tsumo": a player draws a tile
    actor (int): the player who drew the tile
    pai (str): the tile drawn by the player
